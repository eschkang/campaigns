NOVA ROMA — LIGHT SETTING PREMISE (v2.3)

Premise:
An alternate history in which an offshoot of the Roman Republic reaches North America and
establishes an ACTUAL Roman-style republican colony on the southern tip of Manhattan Island
around 509 BC. This is not "Roman aesthetic" fantasy: institutions, language, and norms are
explicitly modeled on the early Republic and adapted pragmatically to local conditions.

Population at start:
- ~2,000 people total (including citizens, clients, non-citizen residents, dependents, and bonded labor).
- Initial settlement footprint concentrated in Lower Manhattan (Battery region), expanding northward.

Language & records:
- Administrative language: Latin (with practical loanwords for local geography/materials).
- Written records exist and are used for laws, censuses, contracts, and engineering notes.
- Literacy is uneven but present among magistrates, scribes, merchants, and priests.

Government (high level):
- Roman republican framework: magistrates, senate, assemblies (adapted for a small colony).
- Senate exists as a deliberative body of leading households (size to be determined by campaign rules),
  advising magistrates on law, war, trade, and public works.
- Customs and civic religion broadly Roman: public rites, calendars, oaths, patronage, and legalism.

Economy & customs:
- Mediterranean-style household production + trade/logistics focus.
- Customs duties are levied at docks/landings; permits and tariffs exist for controlled goods.
- Standard weights/measures are used; coinage may be limited early and supplemented by bullion and ledgers.

Technology level (hard constraints):
- Baseline tech = late Iron Age / early Roman Republic capabilities.
- Allowed: ironworking (bloomeries), simple steel (limited), carpentry, masonry, lime mortar,
  pottery/brick, rope/sailmaking, basic hydraulics, roadbuilding, surveying, navigation by sun/stars,
  simple machines (pulleys, winches), animal power, watermills ONLY after suitable build-out and know-how.
- NOT allowed (hard constraint): electricity generation, internal combustion, steam power, gunpowder,
  advanced chemistry (industrial acids/alkalis at scale), modern optics, germ theory medicine,
  precision machining, semiconductors, modern explosives, telegraph/radio.
- “Research” can exist, but breakthroughs must be plausible from this baseline and constrained by:
  (1) available materials, (2) energy sources, (3) tool precision, (4) labor organization, (5) time.

Tone & focus:
- Plausible-alt-history rather than mythic fantasy.
- Emphasis on logistics, surveying, governance, trade, diplomacy with Indigenous polities,
  and incremental infrastructure.
- Exploration and negotiation matter as much as warfare.

This file is intentionally compact: it defines the premise + constraints only.
All further institutions, factions, and timelines should be generated consistently with this starting point
and the accompanying grid/shoreline canon.

-----END-----

# macro_cell_helper.py
# Compute MGRD-300 macro indices from a base 100m grid coordinate.
# Usage examples:
#   python macro_cell_helper.py 10 12
#   python macro_cell_helper.py 255 301

import sys
if len(sys.argv) != 3:
    print("Usage: python macro_cell_helper.py <base_row_int> <base_col_int>")
    raise SystemExit(2)

r = int(sys.argv[1]); c = int(sys.argv[2])
mr = r // 3
mc = c // 3
print(f"Base {r:03d}-{c:03d} -> Macro MR={mr:03d}, MC={mc:03d} (MGRD-300)")

-----END-----

TECH CONSTRAINTS (v2.3)

Baseline: Late Iron Age / early Roman Republic (circa 509 BC), adapted to local materials.

Hard NOT-ALLOWED:
- Electricity generation or distribution
- Steam power (boilers/engines)
- Internal combustion engines
- Gunpowder / firearms / modern explosives
- Modern industrial chemistry (large-scale acids/alkalis, petroleum refining)
- Telegraph/radio, electronics, semiconductors
- Precision machining beyond period capability
- Germ theory dependent medicine, vaccines as modern practice

Allowed (examples):
- Iron blooms, forging, limited carburized steel
- Carpentry, shipbuilding, rope/sail, navigation by sun/stars
- Masonry, lime mortar, brick/pottery
- Surveying, roadbuilding, aqueduct-style hydraulics
- Simple machines: pulleys, winches, screws
- Animal power; watermills only when plausibly engineered and supplied

Principle:
Any tech advance must be justified by materials + energy + tool precision + organization + time.
